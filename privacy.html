BattleBots Arena Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 4, 2026

BattleBots Arena is a game by Fit Egg. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data Collection

BattleBots Arena does not collect, sell, or share personal information. The game does not require an account, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifier.

Local Game Data

The app stores gameplay progress locally on your device, including items such as unlocks, settings, wins, losses, experience, and scrap. This data stays on your device unless you delete the app or clear its data.

Multiplayer

If you use multiplayer, the app uses network connectivity to connect players for gameplay. Multiplayer may exchange temporary room/session information and gameplay state needed to run the match. BattleBots Arena does not use this information to identify you or build a profile.

Analytics and Tracking

The app does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or cross-app tracking.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated if app features change. The latest version will be posted on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, visit BattleBots Arena Support.
